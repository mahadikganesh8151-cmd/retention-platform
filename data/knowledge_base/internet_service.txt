Internet Service Plans - TelcoRetain

DSL: Available in most areas, speeds up to 50 Mbps. Lower cost, more stable 
in areas with older infrastructure.

Fiber optic: Available in select areas, speeds up to 1 Gbps. Higher cost. 
Known issue: fiber optic customers have historically reported higher churn 
rates, often linked to installation delays and higher monthly pricing 
compared to DSL.

Troubleshooting slow internet: restart your router (unplug for 30 seconds), 
check for outages in your area via the TelcoRetain status page, ensure no 
more than 8 devices are connected simultaneously on standard plans.
