Cancellation Policy - TelcoRetain

To cancel service, customers must contact support at least 3 business days 
before their next billing date. Cancellations are NOT retroactive - customers 
are billed for the full current cycle even if cancelling mid-cycle.

Retention offers: customers requesting cancellation are eligible for one 
retention offer per year, which may include a discounted rate for 3 months 
or a free service upgrade, at the discretion of the support agent handling 
the request.

Equipment return: any rented equipment (routers, modems) must be returned 
within 14 days of cancellation, or a $75 non-return fee is charged.
