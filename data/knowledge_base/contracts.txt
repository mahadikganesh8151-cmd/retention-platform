Contract Types - TelcoRetain

Month-to-month: No long-term commitment. Can be cancelled anytime with no 
penalty, effective at the end of the current billing cycle.

One-year contract: Discounted monthly rate compared to month-to-month. 
Early cancellation before the 12-month term incurs a $150 early termination fee.

Two-year contract: Lowest available monthly rate. Early cancellation before 
the 24-month term incurs a $250 early termination fee, prorated down after 
month 12.

Customers can switch from month-to-month to a one or two-year contract at 
any time to access lower rates, but cannot switch from a longer contract to 
month-to-month before the term ends without paying the early termination fee.
